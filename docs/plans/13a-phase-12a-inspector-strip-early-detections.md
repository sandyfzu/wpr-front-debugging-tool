# Phase 12A — The on-page detection widget, early start (the Known Conflicts shell)

**Depends on:** P1/P1.1 (detection engine + its verified labels), P3 (data hub; the injected-UI mount/theme stack), P0 (tokens) — and the Known Conflicts redesign (commit `bf97ab7`), whose shell pattern and audited motion/focus choreography are adopted verbatim. **No** P11/P5-panel dependency. **Effort:** S–M (four sub-phases, ~3 days). **Parallelizable:** with every phase that does not touch `entrypoints/isolated.content/**`, `lib/detection/describe` strings, or the context-menu registry; P6/P8/P9/P10 surfaces are untouched by design.

## Owner mandate (2026-10-13)

The on-page inspector widget presents itself with the **Known Conflicts widget's UI**, adapted to what the strip needs — and the early start ships **detections only**. Everything else in the planned P12 scope (drag + persisted position, `Alt+H`, the tool chips and their outcome banners, the popup/Detections/options opt-in row itself, the hostile-CSS formal pass) keeps its planned slot in P12/P13/P14.

**Owner addendum (2026-10-13, later the same day):** dragging joins the early start as **P12A.5** — the chrome is draggable with immediate repositioning and the position is **session-local only** (no storage write); the persisted position, `Alt+H`, the tool chips, the opt-in rows and the hostile-CSS formal pass keep their planned slots.

## TL;DR

A second injected widget joins the two shipped ones: a **mood-driven marker pill** (identity: `WPR <version>` or `WPR not found`; status chip: `✓ Cached` + timestamp / `Not cached` / pending pulse / pass-failure) resting at bottom-right 16/16, expanding into a **floating report card** (eyebrow per the brief's header-row semantics, one scroll region, grouped evidence rows with state chips and honest tooltips, footer micro-info), on the same class-driven visibility/opacity/transform choreography as KC — including the audit hardening: the held-visible departing leg, the machinery-free card under reduced motion, and the attempt→verify→re-arm `moveFocus`. Detections come exclusively from the data hub's already-cached single pass — no second detection pass, ever. Chips conform to the P5H brief's state vocabulary (`SB-D5`/`SB-D6`) and ride plugin-native labels straight from `describe.ts`'s verified set.

### P12A.5 — Drag (joins the early start; session-local only)

**Depends on:** P12A.2. **Parallelizable:** —.

**Owner decision (2026-10-13):** dragging joins the P12A early start; persistence stays with full P12. The chrome — the pill, or the anchored open card via its non-interactive chrome regions — is draggable with immediate repositioning; the position is **session-local only**: no storage write, no per-origin state, position resets on navigation/reload to the bottom-right 16/16 anchor. `SB-D12`'s drag semantics minus its persistence half, over [06c](./06c-phase-5-strip-design-brief.md)'s `SB-D12`/`SB-D16` (grab/grabbing cursor, 1:1 follow, clamp, never selects host text; no page scroll).

Design decisions (recorded; the machine and the wiring each pin their half):

- **Drag handles** — the pill (whole; a press-and-move threshold separates drag from its open-the-card click, whose trailing click a moved drag suppresses) and the open card's header/footer non-interactive regions. The card's body scroll region is deliberately not a handle (its gestures are the scroll's); the close control never starts a drag (`SB-D12`'s interactive-elements rule).
- **One draggable element, both shapes** — the brief's geometry (`SB-D1` inner fixed chrome; pill and card both anchor bottom-right inside it) makes the chrome the single draggable element: dragging moves the chrome, the open card rides it, and open/close never snaps back to the resting anchor within the session (the card follows the pill's anchor trivially — one anchor exists; this sub-phase picks nothing else).
- **Cancel** — Escape, `pointercancel`, a lost pointer capture, and any pointerdown received mid-drag (right-click = the brief's source-parity nicety; a second pointer = the multi-touch conflict) all cancel and restore the pre-drag position; a canceled move-suppresses the trailing pill click.
- **Clamp** — **full viewport containment (owner binding, the 2026-10-14 feedback round): the dragged shape stays entirely inside the viewport, `DRAG_TUNING.containmentMarginPx` = 4 px of air inside every edge, in both states, at rest and during drag** (the owner: "not outside at all" — no part may sit offscreen; the parked-grabbable strip figure is obsolete: hiding is the context-menu toggle's, and the future keyboard shortcut's, job). Wheel/scroll during a drag: **clamp-and-follow** — positions are viewport coordinates re-clamped against the live viewport on every move, and the fixed chrome holds its viewport position with no scroll handling at all (no `preventDefault` anywhere; `touch-action: none` on the handles keeps a touch drag from becoming a page pan — `SB-D16`).
- **Keyboard** — none, recorded: the brief grants drag to pointers (`SB-D12` is pointer semantics); keyboard users keep the anchor and the focus choreography; Escape-at-rest stays the shipped close-card path.
- **Cursor** — `cursor: grab` on the card handles + the whole chrome `grabbing` during a drag; the pill keeps its `cursor: pointer` (its primary affordance is the card toggle — the drag is press-and-move) and its press/hover theatrics stand down during a drag (`SB-D12`: no scale/opacity theatrics while dragging).
- **Reduced motion** — untouched by design: dragging is direct manipulation, no transition on position exists on the chrome, the open/close choreography keeps riding the tokens.

Tasks:

- `entrypoints/isolated.content/inspector/drag.ts` — the pure drag machine (`createDragController`: grab → bounded follow → settle/cancel, injected viewport/box/apply seams, the click-suppression read-and-clear) plus the exported pure `clampDragPosition`; the slop/clamp bounds as a `DRAG_TUNING` data table the module owns.
- `entrypoints/isolated.content/inspector/index.tsx` — per-mount controller (a fresh mount starts at the resting anchor — the session reset is the mount path), pointer capture on the grabbed shape, listeners bound to the chrome through `ctx` (never page-wide), Escape arms cancel-while-dragging then the shipped close.
- `entrypoints/isolated.content/inspector/inspector.css` — the chrome's drag-translate custom properties (declared at 0 px in the chrome's own block so the injected graph defines every property it consumes; inline writes override), the grab/grabbing affordance legs, `touch-action: none` on the handles.

Tests: the machine unit-pinned in `drag.test.ts` (grab/dead-chrome, the (3, 4)-triangle slop boundary, clamped follow at all four edges, negative-range inversion, settle/cancel/restore, click suppression, wrong-button + second-pointer conflicts, mid-drag card growth + viewport shrink re-clamp, stale-pointer immunity, the keyboard/pointer click discriminator); e2e legs in `tests/e2e/suites/site-inspector-strip.spec.ts` (pill drag repositions + never opens the card + reload returns the resting anchor; card drag + the pill re-appears at the dragged anchor after close; edge parked keeps the visible strip and no page scroll — the `SB-D16` pin; Escape mid-drag restores the pre-drag position; the keyboard stays dead-end-free after a card-handle drag — pointer-only suppression). No keyboard pin (recorded above). The multi-touch conflict is unit-pinned only — the tier drives one pointer.

**DoD:** gates green (compile, lint, format:check, `pnpm test`, `pnpm build` + `check:budgets` with the isolated-CS row re-derived for the drag weight, full `pnpm test:e2e`, `build:firefox`); receipts in §7 (the adversarial audit round runs after this sub-phase).
